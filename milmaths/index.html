MilMATHS

Four stages. Forty lessons. Four tests. One milbil who sits with you the whole way.

Each stage teaches a harder piece of the Australian Curriculum than the one before — place value, then times tables and fractions, then decimals and measuring, then algebra. Every question you answer earns XP, and XP buys milbils in the shop or parts to build one of your own. Finish a lesson and the next one opens; pass the stage test and the next stage does.
