Milbil Hotel

How to play

🛎️

Check guests in. Milbils, Petal, Chomp, Hattie, Sunny and Scoop wait in the lobby. Tap one and they head up to a room.

🪙

Get paid. When they check out, tap the coins over their room.

🧹

Tidy up. Tap the broom so the room is ready for the next guest.

🛠

Build. More rooms, new floors, a café, a spa, a pool on the roof. Guests whose wish comes true pay more.

🖐️

Getting around. Drag to go up and down the hotel, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Milbil Town is full of visitors and they all need somewhere to sleep. You have a lobby, two little rooms and a bell on the desk.
