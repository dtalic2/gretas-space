Milbils walk on at the far row and hop toward you. One that hops off the near row reaches the tower and takes a shield. Lose every shield and the Milbils have the tower.

Round 1 has one Milbil, round 2 has two, and so on. Clear them all to move on. Line two up in a single beam for a bonus.
