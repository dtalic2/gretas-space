Milbil Books

Make your own comic books, starring milbils.

Pull the panels on a page to the size you want, put milbils in them, give them speech bubbles, and draw anything you like in any colour. The pages number themselves. When a book is finished, tap ✓ Done and it goes on the shelf in the Library — and you can open it up and ✏️ Edit it again whenever you like.
