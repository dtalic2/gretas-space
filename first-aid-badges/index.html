    Printing on the A1 mini

    With the AMS lite

    1. Open the .3mf in Bambu Studio. Pick the Bambu Lab A1 mini, 0.20mm Standard, and the Textured PEI plate.
    2. Put white PLA in AMS slot 1 and green in slot 2.
    3. Slice. In the preview, drag the layer slider to layer 11 (2.20 mm), click the + next to it and choose Change filament → 2.
    4. Print. It swaps to green once, for the top millimetre.

    Without an AMS

    1. Open the .3mf in Bambu Studio and slice it with white loaded.
    2. In the preview, go to layer 11 (2.20 mm), right-click the + and choose Add pause.
    3. Print. When it pauses after the white plate, unload the white, load the green from the printer's screen, and resume.

    Each piece is two parts, base and top, so you can also set the colours per part in Bambu Studio's object list instead of at a layer. The STL files are one solid each, for any other slicer.

    Wearing them

    The backs are flat, so a stick-on or glued bar pin (25–30 mm) works, or a 45 × 13 mm magnetic badge back for clothes you don't want pinned.

    The Cadet tag is a solid green bar and the Junior tag is white with a green frame, so the two are easy to tell apart across a room.

    The cross is the green and white first aid sign. The red cross on white is a protected emblem, so it isn't used here. The files are made by tools/make_badges.py: change the names at the top and run it again for more badges.
