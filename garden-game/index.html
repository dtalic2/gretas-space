🌻 How to play

  1. Tap or click any plot to tend it — you don't have to walk over. Point at one to see how grown its fruit is.
  2. Walk with WASD, the arrow keys or the on-screen stick. Drag to look around; pinch with two fingers or scroll to zoom.
  3. Pick a seed from the hotbar — click it, press 1–9, or tap Q to cycle. Then tap a bare plot to plant it.
  4. Crops get thirsty halfway through — a 💧 appears. Tap to water them and they grow twice as fast.
  5. When a crop sparkles ✨ it's ripe. Tap to harvest it for coins.
  6. Trees and vines are never dug up — you pick their fruit and the plant stays, growing more.
  7. Spend coins at the Seed Shop — a new variety unlocks at nearly every level, twenty in all. The Animal Pen stocks animals that speed up crops, raise sale prices or improve your luck, and the Carpenter sells extra soil plots and decorations.
  8. The Market has one seed heavily discounted at a time. The glowing Magic Tree sells eleven rare seeds you can't buy anywhere else, plus permanent charms that speed up the whole garden.
  9. Rich enough? Buy a Magic Sapling at the Magic Tree for your own free-seed tree, then move on to the 🔮 Magic Market, the 🌟 Super Magic Tree (100 mythic plants) and the 🌌 Enchanted Realm the 🐲 Dragon Cave, the 🏰 Magic Mansion and finally the ♾️ Infinity Void.
  10. The Carpenter also sells tools: a 🚿 Sprinkler, a 🧺 Harvest Basket and a 🌾 Seed Drill. Own any of them and an ⚡ button appears that tends the whole garden in one press.
  11. Bought the 🪏 Shovel? Toggle it (or press G) and tap a plant to dig it up for half its seed price — the only way to free a plot a tree is sitting on.
  12. Press B or the 🎒 button to see everything you own, and ⚙️ to change the animation style.

Your garden keeps growing while you're away, so come back to a full harvest.
