Filling the valley…

Ten dollars and nine days of rain

The river came over its banks on Monday. It has not stopped raining since. By the tenth night the whole valley goes under — the ridge, the road, the roof of your house.

💵

You have $10. That buys a bag of nails.

🪵

Materials are free — driftwood, scrap, clay, reeds — but the water takes the low ground first.

🪧

Build a business. Turn what you gather into things your neighbours are desperate for, and sell it at your stall.

🏠

Then buy timber and either raise your house onto stilts or build a boat. You probably cannot afford both.

⚠️ Cedar Ridge is the highest ground in the valley at 8.4 m. The surge comes in at 10.5 m. Standing still is not a plan.
