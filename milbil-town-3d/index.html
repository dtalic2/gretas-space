Milbil Town

How to play

🌾

Plant a field. Tap it, pick a crop, wait, tap again to harvest.

🥐

Build a workshop. Wheat becomes bread, berries become cake.

🚁

Mail to the helipad. Visitors land wanting things — they pay far better than selling raw.

🏡

Build homes. Every workshop needs milbils to run it.

🖐️

Getting around.

Milbils are small, round and never happier than when there is something to do. You have an island, a barn, a helipad and three of them. Grow things, make things, mail them off, and the island fills up.

The ⚙️ cog has all of this again, plus the keyboard shortcuts, whenever you want it.
