Lunchbox Simulator

You pack the school lunchboxes.

Every morning one milbil hands you their lunchbox, their lunch money and a note saying what they'd like. Buy the food at Bill's, Billy's or Lebelibs — they're all after your money, so the prices change every day — and pack the box. At lunchtime three judges mark it out of ten each, and you walk home with up to 30 points and whatever change is left.
