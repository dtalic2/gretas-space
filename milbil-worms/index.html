How to play

Two teams of Milbils, one map that blows apart. Wipe out the other side. Each turn you get 30 seconds to walk, aim and fire — then it is their go.

The longer you drag, the harder the shot — and the further your finger is from the Milbil, the finer the angle gets. The dotted line shows where the shot starts out; wind does the rest, and the bar at the top says which way it is pushing.

For the fiddly ones, use ▲ and ▼ next to FIRE to dial the angle in a fraction of a degree at a time, then hold FIRE and let go at the power you want. The faint tick on the power ring is where your last shot went off, so "same again, a bit harder" is easy.

Pinch out — or tap ⤢ — to see the whole board, and the zoom stays where you put it.

Things that will kill you

Falling a long way. The water. Standing next to a team-mate's rocket — friendly fire is on, for you and for the computer.

Anything with a fuse gives you the time back to get away from it: drop dynamite and the screen says RUN! — walk and jump still work until it goes off.

Crates

A crate drops most turns. Walk into it: weapon crates restock the rare stuff, health crates patch you up 30.

Sudden death

Nine rounds in, everyone drops to 25 health and the water starts climbing a little every turn. It ends games.
