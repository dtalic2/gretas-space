How to play

  1. Every nest has a giant asleep beside it. Walk over and press E to take an egg.
  2. The giant is listening. The faster you move the more noise you make — crouch to creep, and stand near a tree or rock to muffle it. The ring over its head is how close it is to waking.
  3. The moment you lift an egg, it wakes up and chases you. Run.
  4. Your pen is the fenced ring in the middle. Giants stop at the gate. Get inside and you are safe.
  5. Caught outside? You only drop the egg. Nothing else is lost, so it is always worth a try.
  6. Stand on the stump in your pen and press E to hatch. Answer the question and the egg cracks open.
  7. Your animals live in the pen and earn coins every minute. Spend coins on boots, quiet socks, a bigger basket, and new places to raid.
